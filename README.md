## 🌍 Live Translation

Translation works alongside transcription. Once captions are appearing, you can choose the language you want to read. This is helpful when the spoken audio is in one language and you are more comfortable reading another.

Keep your expectations realistic. Translation appears as speech is recognized, so it arrives in short pieces rather than as a finished paragraph. Slight delays are normal. Short, clear sentences translate more smoothly than rapid, overlapping conversation.

## 👥 Speaker Labels

Speaker labels help you tell participants apart in a conversation. When two or more people speak, the captions can indicate a change of speaker. This is most useful in meetings, interviews, panel discussions, and recorded conversations with multiple voices.

Speaker labels work best when each person speaks clearly and does not talk over others. If two people speak at the same time, the labels may be less precise for that moment.

## 🛠️ Tips for Clearer Captions

- Play audio at a normal volume; very quiet audio produces weaker results.
- Reduce background noise such as fans, music, or open windows.
- Use headphones to prevent echo when using a microphone.
- Ask people to speak one at a time during meetings.
- Keep sentences reasonably short.
- Pause the session if you need to step away.

## ❓ Troubleshooting Common Problems

**The application does not open.** Open the file again from your Downloads folder. If Windows shows a security prompt, choose the option that allows the application to run.

**No captions appear.** Confirm that audio is playing and that the correct audio source is selected. Check that your system volume is not muted.

**Captions appear but are inaccurate.** Move closer to the sound source, reduce background noise, and make sure speech is clear. Accuracy improves when one person speaks at a time.

**Translation lags behind.** A short delay is normal because translation follows recognition. Wait a few seconds and the text should catch up.

**Captions stop suddenly.** The session may have been paused, the audio may have stopped, or the connection may have dropped. Restart the session and try again.
